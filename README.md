## 🧬 About Us

Welcome to **KITTY × TWEAK LABS (KTL)**, an independent collaborative research and development initiative founded by **KrazyKitty** and **Tweak**.

We bring together two complementary engineering ecosystems to explore artificial intelligence, language models, software engineering, cybersecurity, GPU computing, operating systems and distributed infrastructure.

Our mission is to transform ambitious technical ideas into reproducible experiments, validated research, reliable software and open-source contributions.

We believe meaningful innovation begins with curiosity, experimentation, transparency and strong engineering practices.

## 🏗️ Our Engineering Philosophy

**Research before assumptions.**  
Every major technical decision should be supported by evidence.

**Reproducibility over unsupported claims.**  
Experiments should be documented, repeatable and independently reviewable.

**Security by design.**  
Protecting infrastructure, research artifacts and development environments is fundamental.

**Collaboration without losing independence.**  
Both researchers retain ownership of their individual projects while contributing to explicitly agreed joint research.

**Quality over quantity.**  
A validated prototype is more valuable than several unfinished implementations.
